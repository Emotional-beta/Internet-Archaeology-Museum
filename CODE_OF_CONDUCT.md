# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our Pledge / 我们的承诺

We want the Internet Archaeology Museum to be a respectful and welcoming place for everyone interested in Internet history, technology, culture, and digital memories.

我们希望互联网考古博物馆成为一个尊重且包容的地方，欢迎所有对互联网历史、技术、文化和数字记忆感兴趣的人。

Participants are expected to behave respectfully and constructively.

参与者应以尊重和建设性的方式行事。

---

## Expected Behavior / 期望的行为

Examples of positive behavior include / 积极行为的示例：

- Respecting different perspectives / 尊重不同观点
- Giving constructive feedback / 提供建设性反馈
- Accepting reasonable disagreement / 接受合理的分歧
- Helping other contributors / 帮助其他贡献者
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people / 将批评集中在想法和代码上，而不是人身上
- Respecting historical and cultural differences / 尊重历史和文化差异

---

## Unacceptable Behavior / 不可接受的行为

The following behavior is not acceptable / 以下行为不可接受：

- Harassment / 骚扰
- Personal attacks / 人身攻击
- Discrimination / 歧视
- Threats / 威胁
- Deliberate intimidation / 蓄意恐吓
- Publishing private information without consent / 未经同意发布私人信息
- Malicious disruption / 恶意干扰
- Trolling intended to prevent constructive discussion / 以阻止建设性讨论为目的的挑衅行为

---

## Historical Discussion / 历史讨论

Internet history can involve controversial companies, communities, technologies, and events.

互联网历史可能涉及有争议的公司、社区、技术和事件。

Disagreement is acceptable. Personal attacks are not.

分歧是可以接受的，人身攻击不可接受。

Historical claims should be discussed using evidence whenever possible.

历史陈述应尽可能基于证据进行讨论。

---

## Enforcement / 执行

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a safe and constructive community.

项目维护者可以在必要时删除内容或限制参与，以维护一个安全和建设性的社区。
